# 0. Intro

> Across the course of his intellectual life, Peirce
> continually returned to and developed his ideas about
> signs and semiotic and there are three broadly delineable
> accounts:
> - a concise Early Account from the 1860s;
> - a complete and relatively neat Interim Account developed
>   through the 1880s and 1890s and presented in 1903;
> - and his speculative, rambling, and incomplete Final
>   Account developed between 1906 and 1910.

> However, despite these changes, Peirce’s ideas on the
> basic structure of signs and signification remain largely
> uniform throughout his developments.

# 1. Basic Sign Structure

> I define a sign as anything which is so determined by
> something else, called its Object, and so determines an
> effect upon a person, which effect I call its
> interpretant, that the later is thereby mediately
> determined by the former. (EP2, 478)
>
> -- Peirce

符号过程（Semiosis）中有三个元素：符号、对象、解释。
一个符号指向了一个对象，是就某个人的理解而言的，
之所以有这种指向关系，是因为这个人做出了这样的解释。
人产生的解释，也是符号对这个人的作用（Effect）。

# 2. Peirce’s Early Account: 1867–8.

> Peirce’s earliest significant attempt at an account of
> signs comes in his 1867 paper “On A New List of
> Categories” (W2 .49–58).

这里已经有符号三分法的体现了。

所谓「注意力所感知到的整体」是：

> ... the conception of what is present in general, is the

## 2.1 Thought-Signs

> An interesting feature of Peirce’s early account is that
> he is keen to associate signs with cognition. In
> particular, Peirce claims that all thought is in signs
> (W2. 213). We can see this from Peirce’s early idea that
> every interpretant is itself a further sign of the
> signified object. Since interpretants are the interpreting
> thoughts we have of signifying relations, and these
> interpreting thoughts are themselves signs, it seems to be
> a straight-forward consequence that all thoughts are
> signs, or as Peirce calls them “thought-signs”.

## 2.2 Infinite Semiosis

> ... interpretants are to count as further signs, and signs
> are interpretants of earlier signs. Since any sign must
> determine an interpretant in order to count as a sign, and
> interpretants are themselves signs, infinite chains of
> signs seem to become conceptually necessary.

作者似乎认为无尽的符号过程是不妥的。

但是这很切合 AI agent 的工作方式，
因为 AI agent 的符号过程是没有起点的，
它起源于训练集中的文本数据，
而这些文本信息几乎是人类产生的所有文本数据。
这个过程也是没有终点的，
当 AI agent 停下来时，它是在等待人类的输入，
而人类的输入也是对上下文（context）中的符号的解释。

可以说，是 AI agent 和人类共同完成了这个无尽的符号过程。
